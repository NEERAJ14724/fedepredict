Source

Estimate

Delay probability is the observed ratio of arrivals delayed 15 minutes or more to arriving flights in the matched historical cohort. Average delay is total arrival delay divided by arriving flights.

Federation

The interactive example demonstrates sample-weighted averaging of client summary statistics. It is not secure aggregation, differential privacy, or a trained LSTM–GCN neural network.
